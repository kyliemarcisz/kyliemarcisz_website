MNIST Quantization
Float64 → Float32 → Float16 → Int8
A deep dive into model compression — taking a hand-trained MLP classifier on MNIST and systematically compressing its weights through every precision level. Shipped as a Flask web app so anyone can upload a digit and see the model's confidence shift across precisions in real time.

NLP · Transformer Compression
BERT Quantization
DistilBERT Float32 → Int8 · Sentiment Analysis
Dynamic post-training quantization of DistilBERT for movie review sentiment classification. Used PyTorch's quantization toolkit to convert all linear layers to int8 — benchmarked against the float32 baseline on accuracy, model size, and inference latency. 4× smaller, near-identical performance.
